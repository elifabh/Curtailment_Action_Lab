The weather model beats all listed comparators on point estimates. Its MAE difference versus the zero forecast has a 95% block-bootstrap interval of −45.38 to +19.76 MW, which includes zero. Robust superiority over zero is not established.

This does not demonstrate avoided curtailment. Selecting six hours using this model did not beat the calendar comparator in the historical time-alignment diagnostic.

What was tested

  • Fixed model trained on January–June 2026; evaluated on August. July was unused.
  • Issued at 12:00 UTC on the preceding day, using system measurements no later than 11:00 UTC.
  • Archived ECMWF 100 m wind forecasts from eight representative locations, using the documented 48-hour lead-time offset.
  • Fixed local gradient-boosting model with 78 features. No Microsoft AI service is connected.

What remains unproven

  • August was previously inspected: this is not an independent holdout or an as-issued operational test.
  • Historical forecast delivery times, original measurement vintages and training-label release dates were not audited.
  • National forecasts cannot establish the effect of an individual site. Cause-specific models did not pass the comparison gate.
  • No saved MWh, trading profit or field impact is claimed. August prices are not included.
