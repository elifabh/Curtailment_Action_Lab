# Criteria status — v4

The published criteria are **Innovation, Scalability, Technical difficulty, Presentation, Deemed success, and Business/Environmental impact**. This is an evidence assessment, not a predicted jury score. Source checked 27 September 2026: https://hacktheclimate.io/

The published mission permits deployable products, decision-support tools and optimisation engines using real energy data and Microsoft AI. It does not state that a completed field pilot is mandatory. Our real input is a public electrical planning model; added wind/flexible demand are declared assumptions. Live Microsoft AI use still needs event access and verification.

| Criterion | What this package now supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Innovation | A traceable workflow combining location screening, source-based numerical verification, failed-case handling and a conditional economic threshold. A primary-source comparator review is supplied. | Flexibility, power flow and AI tool calling already exist. No world-first or proven competitive advantage claim. |
| Scalability | A workload on a 2,025-bus source; 60 paired conditions, eight candidate connections and recorded solver counts, time and memory. Simple baselines are included. | One prepared network and one machine do not prove real-time utility-wide performance or transfer to other grids. |
| Technical difficulty | RAW conversion corrections; source-bounded controls; AC calculations; independently written source equations; recovered ideal-tie flows; fail-closed candidate/AI/value interfaces. | Full dynamics, remote controls, operational N−1 security and device execution are not implemented. |
| Deemed success | Reproducible equal-demand comparisons, zero-effect controls, two line states, capacity/wind variation and demand stress tests. Positive, neutral and blocked outcomes are retained. | No guarantee for every condition, national Irish reduction, approved dispatch instruction or field trial. |
| Business/Environmental impact | Named user and decision: screen where flexibility helps and the activation premium an assumed event could support. Negative economics and insufficient energy are explicitly handled. | Actual site flexibility, contract prices, customer profit, field MWh, willingness to pay and emissions are not measured. |
| Presentation | Four English tabs, standalone demo, comparison/export flows and the in-app result view are available. | Current slides and rehearsal are deliberately deferred; external presentation files remain the old version. |

## Separate production path

Operator-reviewed ratings and permissions, time-indexed real customer flexibility, security checks and a metered pilot are needed before making operating or field-saving claims. They are production evidence needs, not additional hackathon entry requirements asserted by this project. A promising conditional demonstration and a production-certified product are different completion scopes.
