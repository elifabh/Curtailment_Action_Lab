# Judge Q&A — current v5

**Is this real energy data?** The network source is EirGrid's public Winter Peak 2024 planning model. Customer offers, additional wind and event economics are hypothetical. It is not a replay of metered curtailment events.

**How much actual curtailment have you prevented?** No field total is established. We demonstrate a conditional increase in modelled wind acceptance: 20.703125 MW between the existing and best tested location in the nominal prepared snapshot. This is not a national percentage or an annual MWh result.

**Why can 20 MW of relocated load change wind by more than 20 MW?** The comparison changes power-flow patterns and binding limits, with balancing generation adjusted and equal demand within each pair. The result is not the energy content of the flexible load and cannot be scaled linearly into a national saving.

**Did you remove violations just to get a positive result?** The preparation is declared: source-bounded tap adjustments and one hypothetical capacitor disconnection. Original limits are not widened. Original diagnosis, unsuccessful conditions and the higher-demand blocked baseline remain visible. Operator approval is not claimed.

**Does every flexible asset need its own wind cable?** No. The study uses network connection IDs. A real aggregator would identify eligible flexible demand at those locations; the prototype does not establish that such customers or cable arrangements exist there.

**Why not just use existing software?** Power-flow, curtailment and flexibility optimisation already exist. Our application hypothesis is the visible chain from offer and budget to counterexample to withdrawal or replacement. We have not proved superiority to commercial systems.

**Why AI for seven scenarios?** A rule is sufficient for this small catalogue. The AI adapter demonstrates a bounded investigation interface; live performance is not established. We will not claim an AI advantage unless equal-budget evaluation supports it.

**What do the 74 rejected nominal choices prove?** They show that the chosen extra requirements change decisions in an authored fixture grid. Seven switch and 67 withdraw. This is an enforced rule property, not AI accuracy or real-world failure avoidance.

**Can a data centre use it today?** It can be represented as a hypothetical flexibility provider, but no data-centre workload, cooling, UPS reserve, SoC or SLA schedule has been verified. A site contract needs those inputs and reviewed network limits.

**What is the next customer step?** A read-only pilot with an operator/aggregator: reviewed network states, real locatable offer envelopes, multiple operating periods and agreed economic terms. Production integration, settlement and device control are not included in this hackathon prototype.
