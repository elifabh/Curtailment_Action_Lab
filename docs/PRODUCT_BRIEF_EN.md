# Product brief — v5

**Buyer hypothesis:** a grid planning group, or a flexibility aggregator working with that group. Asset owners supply locatable flexible-demand offers. Generation owners can review the wind-acceptance effect; this is not an energy marketplace.

**Job:** identify where a specified flexible demand could increase wind acceptance, subject to an offer budget and selected stress conditions; retain, switch or withdraw a recommendation with the evidence attached.

**Delivered:** conditional public-network study, eight hypothetical fixed-size offers, physical counterexamples, commercial requirement gates, data-priority explanations, bounded Azure audit interface, standalone English demo, local API, exports and reproducible records.

**Differentiation to test:** one trace from offer assumptions to failed conditions to a revised decision. Power flow, robust selection, flexibility marketplaces and LLM falsification already exist. No defensible first-ever algorithm or proven market advantage is asserted.

**Route to a pilot:** replace hypothetical offers with customer-approved telemetry and flexibility envelopes; review operator network inputs and controls; replay multiple real operating periods against accepted baselines; only then evaluate activation and settlement. These are production validation steps, not things silently represented as completed in a hackathon demo.
